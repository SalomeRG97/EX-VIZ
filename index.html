Meraki Club & Residences, located in one of Cartagena’s most exclusive neighborhoods, redefines premium residential living with more than 160 units in a unique architectural structure.

Location: Cartagena, Colombia
Developer: Grupo Valor
Construction: Convel
Investment Fund: Paladin Realty Partners
Project Services: Architectural CGI, Interior & Exterior Renderings, 360 Virtual Tour, 3D Floor Plan
[1920x1080 Success Case Video]

Our EX-VIZ team transformed static 3D studio designs into an interactive, web-based real estate digital twin, enabling active property sales through immersive visualization and optimized digital engagement. Using Unreal Engine technology, we developed Real-Time Virtual Tours that offered buyers personalized experiences while exploring finishes, materials, and perspectives.

From this model, we generated over 300 photorealistic CGI images, complemented by animations and precision drone photography. Each visual asset was crafted with advanced lighting and composition techniques, faithfully conveying Meraki’s refined aesthetic and luxury.

To further elevate quality, we applied advanced path-tracing, achieving exceptional lighting realism that enhanced the project’s sophistication and commercial appeal. The result was an authentic and captivating representation that strengthened Meraki’s market positioning and delivered a world-class visual experience to investors and buyers.
