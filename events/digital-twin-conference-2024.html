Digital Twin International Conference 2024

EX-VIZ Participation

Delegates from EX-VIZ participated in the Digital Twin International Conference 2024 (DigiTwin 2024), a global hybrid summit connecting industrial leaders and academics.

The conference addressed key technical frontiers including 4D BIM synchronization, real-time sensor stream integration, and cloud-delivered spatial computing. EX-VIZ participated in roundtables detailing how remote digital twin tracking reduces site inspection overhead and mitigates construction phase discrepancies.

This event solidified EX-VIZ's commitment to implementing international best practices across all our real estate and construction digital twin deployments.
