Reality Capture & Multi-Sensor Mapping

What we do:
We capture the physical reality of terrain, active construction sites, and infrastructure assets using ultra-high-precision technology to build reliable digital models from day one. We leverage aerial and terrestrial LiDAR for millimeter-accurate geometry, high-resolution photogrammetry for 3D models and orthomosaics, bathymetry for water bodies, and multispectral sensors to analyze vegetation, erosion, and material compositions. All spatial data is georeferenced with ground control and complemented by specialized technical photography in hard-to-reach areas, ensuring total environmental visibility without putting personnel at risk.

Key Features

  • Aerial & Terrestrial LiDAR: High-density point cloud generation with millimeter-level geometric precision.
  • Precision Photogrammetry: High-definition textured 3D mesh modeling and accurate orthomosaics.
  • Bathymetry & Multispectral Sensing: Hydrographic underwater mapping and spectral soil/vegetation health diagnostics.
  • Georeferencing & Technical Photography: Sub-centimeter ground control alignment and access-restricted visual inspection.

Result

Hyper-accurate digital site foundations that eradicate planning uncertainties, eliminate up to 90% of field surprises, optimize early engineering design, and protect personnel safety in high-risk zones.

Construction Progress & Milestone Tracking

What we do:
We monitor real site progress through scheduled drone flights, LiDAR scans, and advanced photogrammetry. We continuously cross-reference actual physical conditions with design benchmarks, detecting schedule or geometry drifts early before they cause budget overruns. Clear executive reports are delivered for developers, auditors, and contractors. 3D models, point clouds, and orthophotos update on a set cadence, while technical timelapses visually capture the project's historical evolution and streamline stakeholder communication.

Key Features

  • Dynamic 3D Models & Point Clouds: Periodic spatial updates to audit site progression over time.
  • As-Built vs. Design Benchmark: Automated detection of schedule lags and geometry deviations.
  • High-Definition Technical Timelapse & Aerial Inspection: Continuous visual logs and safe access to high-risk areas.
  • Consolidated Executive Reporting: Clear, actionable reports tailored for executive boards, contractors, and auditors.

Result

Complete operational visibility with sub-centimeter accuracy, accelerated decision-making, unassailable physical evidence for audits, and significant reduction in rework and delay costs.

BIM-to-Physical Overlay & Progress Dashboards

What we do:
We link architectural, structural, and MEP BIM models (Revit, Navisworks, IFC) with physical field progress inside interactive real-time dashboards connected to executive schedules (MS Project, Primavera). Using sub-centimeter alignment algorithms, we overlay design specifications directly onto real site photos and scans to instantly isolate clashes, out-of-bounds walls, and trade sequence conflicts.

Key Features

  • Precision BIM Overlay: Sub-centimeter photographic and geometric alignment over active site conditions.
  • Automated Conflict Isolation: Automatic highlighting of misplaced walls, pipes, and ductwork.
  • Color-Coded 3D Visualizer: Real-time component tracking showing finished, in-progress, and delayed elements.
  • Multi-Platform Access: Smooth web-based viewing on office desktops, field laptops, tablets, or mobile devices.

Result

Prevention of costly trade collisions before physical installation, complete elimination of rework expenses, and transparent, real-time progress verification for leadership and investors.

Digital Inspection & AI Anomaly Analysis

What we do:
We process captured optical and thermal imagery datasets to identify defects, anomalies, and structural deviations across assets and surfaces. Utilizing Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence, we detect crack patterns, classify severity levels, and quantify affected surface areas. This semi-automated visual inspection, paired with interactive maps and historical deterioration tracking, delivers clear asset health diagnostics for engineering audits and operational planning.

Key Features

  • Semi-Automated Visual & Thermal Scans: Rapid optical scanning across large infrastructures and vertical facades.
  • AI Anomaly Recognition: Computer vision algorithms for automated crack detection, spalling, and surface wear.
  • Severity Grading & Quantified Surface Metrics: Exact square meter and linear measurements for repair budgeting.
  • Georeferenced Interactive Maps & Time-Series: Continuous tracking of structural degradation over time.

Result

Data-driven predictive maintenance planning, intelligent capital allocation for high-priority repairs, and long-term protection of structural asset integrity.

Digital Engineering & Earthworks Optimization

What we do:
We turn raw reality capture data into engineering-grade digital models. We generate Digital Elevation, Surface, and Terrain Models (DEM, DSM, DTM), perform automated cut & fill volume calculations, and develop precise geometric designs for roads, foundations, and earthworks with tolerances under 1%. Fully integrated with Civil 3D, InfraWorks, Revit, and modern BIM workflows to streamline engineering execution.

Key Features

  • DEM, DSM & DTM Terrain Modeling: High-precision elevation models and digital surface meshes.
  • Automated Cut & Fill Volume Calculations: Precise volumetric measurements for excavation, fill, and stockpiles.
  • Geometric Road & Platform Layouts: Engineered spatial design with strict geometric tolerances (<1%).
  • Native Engineering Software Compatibility: Direct integration with Civil 3D, InfraWorks, Revit, and IFC files.

Result

Massive operational cost reductions in heavy machinery and earthmoving, total elimination of volumetric billing disputes, and pinpoint engineering predictability with sub-centimeter accuracy.
