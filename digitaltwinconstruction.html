Progress Tracking Dashboard

Monitor construction activities visually in real time. Track KPIs, completed tasks, and schedule comparisons in a consolidated 3D layout.

Key Features

  • Interactive 3D Visualizer: Color-coded structures showing finished, in-progress, and delayed components.
  • Dynamic Scheduling: Direct connection to MS Project or Oracle Primavera schedules.
  • Cross-device Access: Run and audit the model on tablets, desktop, or mobile devices on-site.

BIM-to-Physical Overlay

Directly align design models against reality. Using photogrammetric algorithms, overlay BIM specifications onto actual construction state visuals to catch discrepancies early.

Key Features

  • Precision Mapping: Sub-centimeter alignment of design objects on site imagery.
  • Conflict Isolation: Automatic highlighting of out-of-bounds walls, pipes, or conduits.
  • Clash Detection: Prevent trade work conflicts by comparing architectural models with mechanical deployments.

Drone Scan Integration

Integrate periodic high-resolution aerial scans directly into the twin. Leverage automatically aligned point-clouds and orthomosaics to map site elevation and volume progress.

Key Features

  • Volumetric Calculations: Automated estimation of excavation earthworks and material piles.
  • Visual Historic Timelines: Easily flip through dates to visually audit the timeline of site progression.
  • Secure Sharing: Export secure 3D snapshots easily to developers or external regulators.
